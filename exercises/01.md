# Exercise 01

The storefront refreshes its catalog every few seconds through a slow legacy
storage client. Refreshing may take 500 ms, but it must not occupy a Tokio
worker while ordinary requests are running.

```bash
cargo hotlap --exercise 01
```

Open the trace and inspect **Longest polls**. Why does a background refresh
appear as one roughly 500 ms poll instead of as an async wait?

<details>
<summary>Hints</summary>

1. A long poll means one call to `Future::poll` kept the worker busy.
2. The task is spawned from `background/mod.rs`, not from a request.
3. Follow `catalog.refresh` to the synchronous storage operation.
4. Take a look at `spawn_blocking` in tokio: https://docs.rs/tokio/latest/tokio/task/fn.spawn_blocking.html

</details>
