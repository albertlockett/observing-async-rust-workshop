# Exercise 04

Product pages can include recommendations from another service. They are
optional: if that service takes longer than 25 ms, the storefront returns the
page without them.

```bash
cargo hotlap --exercise 04
```

Request latency stays near the budget, but the trace shows much more
recommendation work than reaches a response. Compare the `http.request` and
`recommendations.fetch` spans. What happens to a fetch after its request has
already returned?

<details>
<summary>Hints</summary>

1. Watch **Active tasks** during the run.
2. See what the tasks of `recommendations.fetch` spans spend most time doing.
3. Take a look at `tokio::time::timeout` https://docs.rs/tokio/latest/tokio/time/fn.timeout.html, and our usage of it.
4. The `timeout` is waiting on a spawned task, not directly on the fetch future.
